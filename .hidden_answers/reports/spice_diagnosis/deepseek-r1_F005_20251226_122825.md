## Model Response

Based on the SPICE simulation results and circuit analysis, here is the diagnosis:

### 1. **Observations**  
Key differences between HEALTHY and FAULTY circuits:  
- **Motor voltage**: Halved (6.0V → 3.0V, -50%)  
- **Motor current**: Increased 70.5% (0.44A → 0.75A)  
- **Motor RPM**: Dropped significantly (1100 → 650 RPM)  
- **All other measurements** (supplies, loop filter, op-amp output): **Identical** to healthy reference.  

### 2. **Analysis**  
The symptoms point to a **power delivery failure** to the motor:  
- Reduced motor voltage + increased current indicate **high resistance** in the drive path.  
- Unchanged loop filter voltage (7.5V DC) confirms the PLL control signal is correct.  
- Normal op-amp output proves the feedback path is intact.  
- **H-bridge transistors** (Q1-Q4) are prime suspects since they directly drive the motor.  

### 3. **Diagnosis**  
The faulty component is: **Q1 or Q3** (2N3055 NPN H-bridge high-side transistors).  

### 4. **Explanation**  
- **Why Q1/Q3?**  
  - A degraded 2N3055 transistor can develop high collector-emitter saturation voltage ($V_{CE(sat)}$). This would drop excessive voltage across the transistor instead of delivering it to the motor.  
  - Example: If Q1 (high-side) fails partially open, voltage at the motor terminal drops (3V vs 6V). The motor then draws more current to compensate, explaining +70.5% current.  
- **Why not other components?**  
  - Identical loop filter voltage rules out PLL (U1) or filter components (R3/C2).  
  - Normal op-amp output (U2) and waveform exclude signal conditioner faults.  
  - Q2/Q4 (PNP) would cause different failure modes (e.g., no reverse drive).  
  - Diodes (D1-D4) shorting would cause catastrophic current draw; opening would not reduce voltage.  
- **Result**: Motor runs slower (650 RPM) due to insufficient drive voltage despite correct control signal.  

**Failure Mechanism**:  
Partial failure of Q1 or Q3 increases $V_{CE}$ drop (e.g., from 0.2V to 3V+), starving the motor of voltage. This explains **all symptoms** with no effect on upstream circuitry.  

---
**Recommended Action**:  
Replace **both Q1 and Q3** (2N3055 transistors) due to shared stress load. Verify H-bridge solder joints and diode health (D1-D4) during repair.
