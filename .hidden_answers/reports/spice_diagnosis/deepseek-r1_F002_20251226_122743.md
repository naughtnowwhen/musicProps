   - Motor RPM is **600** (vs. healthy 1100)

2. **Analysis**:
   - The 50% motor voltage drop with proportional RPM decrease suggests **only half the H-bridge is operating**
   - Normal loop filter voltage (7.5V) indicates the PLL control loop is functioning correctly
   - Intact op-amp output confirms feedback signal path is healthy
   - Severely reduced current points to **high resistance in motor drive path**

3. **Diagnosis**:  
   The faulty component is: **Q1 (2N3055 NPN transistor)**

4. **Explanation**:
   - Q1 is the high-side transistor for one half of the H-bridge. If it fails **open**, only the complementary half-bridge (Q2/Q4) can drive the motor, resulting in:
     - ≈50% voltage drop (observed 3V vs 6V) due to single-ended drive
     - Severe current reduction (observed 0.03A vs 0.44A) from increased path resistance
     - Proportional RPM loss (600 vs 1100) since motor speed depends on voltage
   - The PLL loop remains locked (normal loop_filter_dc) because the control voltage is correct, but the drive stage can't deliver full power
   - Q1 failure explains all symptoms while being consistent with intact supplies, control signals, and feedback path

*Failure mechanism*: 2N3055 transistors commonly fail open due to thermal stress in H-bridge configurations. This matches the symptom pattern where half the drive stage is inoperative while all upstream signals remain normal.
