### Diagnosis Report

**1. Observations**  
- **loop_filter_dc**: Dropped from 7.5V (healthy) to 6.5V (-13.3%).  
- **loop_filter_ripple**: Increased from 1.6mV to 461mV (+28,712%).  
- **Motor parameters**: Motor voltage (6.0V → 5.5V), current (0.44A → 0.4A), and RPM (1100 → 1050) are all reduced.  
- **Other points**: Supply voltages (v_supply, v_logic) and signal conditioner output (cond_opamp_vpp/waveform) are normal.  

**2. Analysis**  
- **Key symptom**: Excessive ripple on the loop filter output (461mV vs. 1.6mV) combined with reduced DC voltage.  
- **Loop filter function**: R3 (100kΩ) and C2 (10µF) smooth phase detector (CD4046) pulses into stable DC. High ripple indicates failed filtering.  
- **DC voltage drop**: Suggests a current leak or loading effect pulling the control voltage down.  
- **Component failure logic**:  
  - Normal conditioner output rules out feedback path issues (U2, pickup).  
  - Healthy supplies exclude power rail problems.  
  - Electrolytic capacitors (like C2) commonly fail by developing high **Equivalent Series Resistance (ESR)** or becoming **leaky** (partial short), causing both ripple and DC voltage drop.  
  - C3 (100nF bypass) failure would increase high-frequency noise but not 220Hz ripple.  

**3. Diagnosis**  
The faulty component is: **C2**  

**4. Explanation**  
- **Why C2?**  
  - A degraded/leaky C2 creates a **parallel resistive path** to ground, forming a voltage divider with R3. This lowers the DC voltage (7.5V → 6.5V).  
  - The capacitor's loss of capacitance/ESR increase **disables smoothing**, allowing phase detector pulses to pass as massive ripple (461mV).  
- **Consistency with symptoms**:  
  - Lower DC voltage → reduced motor drive → decreased RPM (1100 → 1050), voltage (6.0V → 5.5V), and current (0.44A → 0.4A).  
  - Ripple causes motor speed instability (though RPM is averaged to 1050).  
  - All other measurements (supplies, conditioner output) remain normal since they precede the loop filter stage.  
- **Failure mode**: Electrolytic capacitors (especially from 1979) are prone to drying/leakage. This matches the observed DC drop + ripple spike.  

**Recommended Action**: Replace C2 (10µF electrolytic capacitor) and verify loop_filter DC/ripple return to normal.
